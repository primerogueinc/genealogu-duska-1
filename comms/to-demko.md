If the officiant is Csurgovich, the book may be Greek Catholic, not Roman Catholic. Csurgovich is a Greek Catholic clerical surname in this region, the father is Greek Catholic, and under the 1868 rule a son followed his father's church. The clerk marks the mother and both godparents as Roman Catholic, which is what a Greek Catholic clerk would need to note. I have dropped `DUS:H-015` (the book is Roman Catholic) from 70% to 35%.
