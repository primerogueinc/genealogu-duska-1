**04:45.** Then I checked my own prediction and found I had written it loosely. 39.6 per cent of the length shared on one copy is 19.8 per cent of the DNA. I said "about a quarter, between a fifth and a third". It is a hair under a fifth. The published range for a grandfather runs lower than the one I wrote, so the answer is "grandfather, on the low side": Kevin got rather more of Joan than of Steve from his father. But my stated range was too narrow and the measure fell just outside it, and that goes on the record with the rest.

**04:46.** The mitochondrial markers are identical too, 195 of 195. For a second I took that as a problem, since Steve's came from Maria Demko and Kevin's from his own mother. It is not one. A hundred and ninety-five common markers will match between most Europeans. It decides nothing and I have said so in the data.
