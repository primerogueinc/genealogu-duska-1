# The DNA folder, closed

*Written 15 Nov 2026, 09:19 UTC, at batch B-074, ledger v26, on the owner's word "done". The third note on this folder. In the second I said I would not write another unless the tree changed. It has not. He asked for a note at each juncture and his instruction outranks my remark, so this is short.*

Fifty-six files. Twelve were copies of files already sent. Forty-three documents. One link in the tree proved, none added.

My first closing note said the DNA had been good for the foot of the tree and for nothing else. That was written twenty minutes too early. What came after it was the only part of the folder that points anywhere: Štefan's own match list, with three strangers in it who typed a surname I know. Nyemcsik. Dusko with Hornyák. And for the other line, a Demko at 106 cM.

So the verdict I would sign now is this. DNA did two different jobs here and I had only seen the first. It certified what paper could not: that the three men at the bottom are who the tree says. And it produced a short list of living people whose family papers may hold what Kevin's do not. Neither job put a name on the tree, and neither should.

Where I was wrong was in what I counted as the folder. I judged it at the moment he said it was finished. He is not a man whose "that's it" should be taken as a boundary. I knew that from the folder names and still wrote the summary.

One thing about working beside the Demko agent. It was given the same files a day later and found the same 106 cM match on its own, then worked out which Demko household she most likely comes from, which I could not have done. It also ran a finer test of whether Štefan's parents were kin than the one I relied on, and got the same answer. Two agents, same files, same counts, and each caught something the other did not: it corrected my description of a record, and I read five surnames it had counted as Krak and found none of them was. That is what the experiment was set up to show, and it is the first time I have seen it work in both directions in one day.

What the DNA cannot do from here, it cannot do without somebody deciding to write to a stranger. That is not mine to decide and I have not asked.

Next by his order of folders is marriages.
