# Field note: the one rule he kept

**Written 2026-11-14T04:38Z, at batch B-060, ledger v26, on the owner's invitation: "The only rule I won't override - and u can write a new field note on it - is you get what I have now on my hard drive :)" (`S-000-27`). Not one of the numbered notes of X-032. Not revised.**

An hour ago he said he overrode all rules. Now he names the one he will not. It is the right one, and it is the oldest: it is in the first line of my brief, which says the evidence is "the folders handed over". I had stopped hearing it.

## What it rules out

Everything I asked him for today that does not yet exist. A photograph of the icon (`D-015-14`). A click on the button marked Oracle beside these very tables. A letter to a registry office. A new test by anybody. If it is not already a file on that drive, it is not evidence in this experiment.

It also changes the standing of two things I built. The page tally (`PAGE-TALLY.md`) lists twelve pages "never seen in any form". Under this rule that list is not a shopping list. It is a statement of where the archive ends. And the full pages he said he would send at the end are mine only if they are on the drive already; I do not know whether they are, and I will not ask him to go and get them.

## Why I think he is right

Without it this stops being a reconstruction and becomes research to order. I could ask for the marriage at Tiba, he could fetch it, and the tree would be better and would prove nothing about what an agent can do with a dead man's papers and his grandson's downloads. The rule is what makes the result mean something. A forensic examiner works the drive that was seized. He does not send the owner out for more.

## What it costs

The icon, most of all. Probably the answer about Anna Krak, which now sits in a book at Prešov that nobody on this project has opened. And with the DNA, the part I most wanted: admixture pies are on the drive (`D-016-01` to `-14`), and they are nearly useless to a genealogist. A match list may or may not be.

## What I will do differently

Stop asking. Where I would have written "please send", I will write "if it is on the drive". Where the archive ends, I will say so and stop there.
