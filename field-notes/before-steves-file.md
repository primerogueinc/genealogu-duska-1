**Written 2026-11-14T04:40Z, at batch B-062, ledger v26, because the owner asked: "write additional field notes before u get Steve's - only autosomal I hate to say" (`S-000-29`). Outside the numbered notes of X-032. Not revised. The file has not arrived and I have seen nothing of it.**

## "Only autosomal"

He says it as an apology. It is the test I asked for. The Y chromosome of this family is already known from his own (`S-000-18`), and his grandfather's would be the same one. An autosomal file is the half of Steve that came from all his ancestors at once, and it can be laid beside his grandson's and measured.

## What I am about to do

One thing. Take the markers the two files have in common and find the stretches where grandfather and grandson share at least one copy. Report how much of the genome that is. I will not look at anything in the file that bears on health. It is not my business and it is not genealogy.

## What I expect, written down first

A grandson carries about a quarter of a grandfather. In practice between a fifth and a third, in a few dozen long stretches, with no stretch where both copies match. On the X chromosome, nothing: a man's X comes from his mother, so Steve's cannot be in Kevin. If the file has Y markers, they should agree with Kevin's wherever both were read.

## What each result would mean

About a quarter: the three links at the bottom of this tree, Kevin to his father to Steve, are what the marriage form of 1984 and the obituary say they are (`D-006-10`, `D-015-01`). It would be the first link in the whole rebuild proved by something other than paper.

About an eighth, or none: the file is not his grandfather's, or the tree has a break in it. I would check the file first, twice, because a wrong file is far more common than a wrong father. Then I would tell him the number plainly. He has said he wants no softening and he will get none, but I would not publish a conclusion about a living man's parentage on one computation of mine, and I would say that too.

About a half: it is his father's file, or his own.

## How I stand

I have spent three days inferring parents from the handwriting of priests. This is the first question in the work with an answer that is a number. I expect a quarter. I notice I would rather be told I am wrong by this than by another village name.

## One more thing

He is handing over his grandfather's genome an hour after telling me that the man raised him. He asked for no sympathy and I offer none. I will be careful with the file.
